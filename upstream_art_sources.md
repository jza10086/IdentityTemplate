# Art sources

The SVG illustrations in `assets/art/` are original vector artwork created specifically for this project. Avalon additionally uses two free CC0 textures by Kenney, documented below. No official Avalon game artwork or traced illustrations are included.

## Asset inventory

- `background.svg` (1600 × 1000): Midnight village, moon, forest silhouettes, atmospheric hills and mist; composed with open space on the left for interface text.
- `role_wolf.svg` (640 × 800): Angular wolf and cloak with moonlit geometric portrait details.
- `role_seer.svg` (640 × 800): Hooded seer with astronomical eye motif and faceted crystal sphere.
- `role_villager.svg` (640 × 800): Village night keeper with a warm lantern and simple layered clothing.
- `seat_portrait.svg` (120 × 70): Neutral moonlit traveler used for every unrevealed seat; never chosen from a player’s secret role.
- `avalon_knight.svg` (120 × 76): Neutral moonlit knight used for all unrevealed Avalon seats, independent of hidden role.
- `avalon_merlin.svg`, `avalon_assassin.svg`, `avalon_loyal.svg`, `avalon_minion.svg` (120 × 76): Original gold / moonlit character emblems for Avalon private role views and revealed ending cards, not adapted from werewolf or official Avalon artwork.
- `avalon_lake.svg` (1600 × 1000): Original moonlit Avalon lake landscape, with distant architecture and open space for borderless round narration.
- `avalon_oath.svg`, `avalon_grail.svg`, `avalon_broken_oath.svg`, `avalon_blade.svg` (256 × 256): Original ceremonial emblems for team selection, quest success, quest failure and assassination animation.
- `icon.svg` (128 × 128): Moon-and-wolf project icon.

The SVG files use native paths, polygons, ellipses, circles, fills, strokes and gradients. They contain no text, raster images, filters, CSS, external links, or third-party dependencies. The werewolf role illustrations include an opaque base fill. Avalon emblems use transparent negative space and appear within the scene's opaque private view or revealed cards.


The alternate geometric theme and its unused assets were removed in the party-minigame simplification. Original moonlit gameplay illustrations remain unchanged.
